# Phases 15, 16 & 17 Implementation Guide — Symbolic MNA Circuit Solver, 2D Matrix Canvas Synthesis, Karnaugh Map Minimizer, and Multi-Coordinate Plotter

## Overview of Added Capabilities

Phases 15, 16, and 17 expand **AxioLoom** into electrical engineering (ECE) circuit analysis, spatial handwriting synthesis of 2D matrices and systems of equations, boolean digital logic minimization via Quine-McCluskey & Karnaugh maps, and multi-coordinate polar/parametric function plotting.

---

## 1. Phase 15 — Symbolic Modified Nodal Analysis (MNA) & Mesh Circuit Solver

### 15.1 Parallel Impedance Operator & Component Helpers
- **Parallel Impedance Operator (`\parallel` or `||` or `\|`)**:
  - AST Operator: `MathExpr.Op.PARALLEL` with precedence `22` (between multiplication/division at `20` and exponentiation at `40`).
  - Compiled in `SymjaCompiler` into:
    $$\left(\frac{A \cdot B}{A + B}\right) \implies \text{canonicalSymja: } ((A*B)/(A+B))$$
  - Supported in `FastPlotEvaluator` and `FastNumericPropagator` for $<0.2\text{ms}$ numeric evaluation.
  - Allows writing $Z_{eq} = R_1 \parallel (R_2 + s L) =$ directly on canvas to obtain exact equivalent impedance.
- **Reactive Component Helpers**:
  - `\operatorname{Zc}(C, s)` / `Zc(C, s)` $\to \frac{1}{s \cdot C}$ (capacitive impedance).
  - `\operatorname{Zl}(L, s)` / `Zl(L, s)` $\to s \cdot L$ (inductive impedance).

### 15.2 Symbolic MNA Engine (`domain/circuit/SymbolicMnaSolver.kt`)
- **Netlist Primitives (`CircuitElement`)**:
  - `Resistor(name, nodeA, nodeB, value)` (admittance $Y = 1/R$)
  - `Capacitor(name, nodeA, nodeB, value)` (admittance $Y = s \cdot C$)
  - `Inductor(name, nodeA, nodeB, value)` (admittance $Y = 1/(s \cdot L)$)
  - `CurrentSource(name, nodeFrom, nodeTo, current)`
  - `VoltageSource(name, nodePos, nodeNeg, voltage)`
  - `Vccs(name, outPos, outNeg, ctrlPos, ctrlNeg, transconductance)` (voltage-controlled current source $g_m$)
- **Matrix Formulation ($\mathbf{G} \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{i}$)**:
  - Automatically stamps conductive/admittance matrix $\mathbf{G}$ and excitation vector $\mathbf{i}$ across all non-ground nodes $1 \dots N$ (with ground as node 0).
  - Solves linear system symbolically using Symja's `LinearSolve` to obtain exact rational expressions for node voltages $V_k(s)$ and branch currents $I_{Vj}$.
- **Thevenin & Norton Equivalents**:
  - Across any user-selected port `(nodeA, nodeB)`:
    - Open-circuit voltage $V_{th} = V_A - V_B$.
    - Thevenin impedance $Z_{th}$: Deactivates independent sources ($V = 0, I = 0$) and injects a $1\text{ A}$ test current between `nodeB` and `nodeA`.

### 15.3 Interactive Circuit Solver Card (`ui/canvas/CircuitSolverSheet.kt`)
- Accessible via "Circuit" button in the top navigation bar.
- Multi-line netlist editor with quick presets (Voltage Divider, RC Low-Pass, RLC Band-Pass).
- Real-time display of solved node voltages, source currents, and Thevenin equivalents.

## 2. Phase 16 — Spatial 2D Matrix & System-of-Equations Synthesis

### 16.1 Geometric Bracket & 2D Grid Synthesizer (`domain/clustering/SpatialStructureSynthesizer.kt`)
- **Tall Bracket Detection (`classifyBracket`)**:
  - Detects single strokes with height $H \ge 2.0 \tilde{h}$ and width $W \le 0.65 H$:
    - `BracketKind.LEFT_BRACKET`: Concave right (endpoints right, midpoint left).
    - `BracketKind.RIGHT_BRACKET`: Concave left (endpoints left, midpoint right).
    - `BracketKind.DETERMINANT_BAR`: Straight vertical bar ($W / H \le 0.18$).
    - `BracketKind.LEFT_BRACE`: Curly brace with central point.
- **2D Grid Cell Alignment (`alignGrid`)**:
  - Enclosed cell clusters are projected onto the $Y$-axis into rows ($|\Delta y| \le 0.6 \tilde{h}$) and onto the $X$-axis into columns.
  - Emits synthesized `\begin{bmatrix} ... \end{bmatrix}` for bracketed matrices and `\begin{vmatrix} ... \end{vmatrix}` for determinant bars.
- **Multi-Block System-of-Equations Synthesis (`synthesizeEquationSystem`)**:
  - Automatically groups 2 to 5 vertically stacked equation blocks sharing `=` into a single simultaneous `MathIntent.SystemOfEq` (`\begin{cases}`).
  - Accessible via "Group as System" in `InspectorPanel`.

## 3. Phase 17 — Boolean Logic, Karnaugh Map Minimizer & Multi-Coordinate Plotter

### 17.1 Quine-McCluskey + Petrick's Method Engine (`domain/logic/KarnaughMinimizer.kt`)
- Supports 2, 3, and 4 variables ($A, B, C, D$) with arbitrary minterm lists $\sum m(\dots)$ and optional don't-care lists $d(\dots)$.
- Exact algorithmic reduction:
  - Generates Prime Implicants by grouping and merging Hamming weight terms.
  - Identifies Essential Prime Implicants (EPIs).
  - Uses Petrick's branch-and-bound method to find the exact minimal prime implicant cover.
  - Emits minimal Sum of Products (SOP) and Product of Sums (POS) in LaTeX.
  - Generates full $2^N$-row truth table.

### 17.2 Interactive Karnaugh Map Card (`ui/canvas/KarnaughMapCard.kt`)
- Accessible via "K-Map" button in top toolbar.
- Renders Gray-code order grids ($2 \times 2, 2 \times 4, 4 \times 4$) with row/col labels ($00, 01, 11, 10$).
- One-tap cell toggling ($0 \to 1 \to X \to 0$) with instantaneous minimal equation re-computation.
- Color-coded prime implicant loop chips (Amber `#FFB300`, Gold `#FFD54F`, Crimson `#DC143C`, Cyan `#00E5FF`).

### 17.3 Multi-Coordinate Function Plotter (`ui/canvas/FunctionPlotCard.kt`)
- Upgraded with coordinate mode selection:
  1. **Cartesian ($y = f(x)$)**: Continuous curve with touch cursor, root indicator, pan/zoom.
  2. **Polar ($r(\theta)$)**: Concentric circular grid lines and $30^\circ$ radial spokes with 360-step sampling across $\theta \in [0, 2\pi]$.
  3. **Parametric ($(x(t), y(t))$)**: Trajectory curve when both $x(t)$ and $y(t)$ are present in the symbol graph.
