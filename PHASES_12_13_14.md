# Phases 12, 13 & 14 Implementation Guide — Live Parameter Scrubbing, Control Systems Workbench, Piecewise Calculus, and Offline Handwritten Symbol Calibrator

## Overview of Added Capabilities

Phases 12, 13, and 14 introduce interactive dynamic calculation, control systems frequency and pole-zero analysis, Taylor series & piecewise mathematics, and offline handwriting calibration to **InkMath**.

---

## 1. Phase 12 — Live Parameter Scrubbing & Interactive Canvas Sliders

### 12.1 Fast Numeric DAG Propagator (`domain/dag/FastNumericPropagator.kt`)
- **60fps Numeric DAG Evaluation**: Bypasses the single-threaded Symja CAS engine during slider scrubbing by evaluating dependent AST nodes numerically in topological order in `< 0.2ms`.
- **Gauss-Legendre Quadrature**: Computes numeric definite integrals $\int_a^b f(x) dx$ dynamically using 16-point Gauss-Legendre quadrature.
- **Topological Sorting**: Dependency analysis propagates parameter changes through downstream assignments and evaluation queries while formatting results to 4 significant digits.
- **150ms Debounced CAS Commit**: Avoids thread starvation by emitting instantaneous numeric previews during dragging, followed by a debounced full symbolic pass to update the reactive symbol graph.

### 12.2 Interactive Parameter Slider Pill (`ui/canvas/ParameterSliderPill.kt`)
- **Dynamic Auto-Bounds**:
  - For $v_0 == 0.0$: Range `[-10.0, 10.0]` with step `0.1`.
  - For $v_0 \ne 0.0$: Dynamically bounds to $[\min(-2|v_0|, 0.1 v_0), \max(2|v_0|, 2 v_0)]$.
- **1-Tap Preset Range Chips**: Immediate range switching to `[-10, 10]`, `[0, 100]`, and `[0, 2π]`.
- **60fps Play / Oscillate Toggle (`▶` / `⏸`)**: Smoothly animates the variable continuously across its range at 60fps to visualize dynamic graph and plot morphing.

## 2. Phase 13 — Control Systems & Signal Analysis Workbench

### 13.1 Transfer Function Detection & Analysis (`domain/cas/TransferFunctionAnalyzer.kt`)
- **Rational Transfer Function Detection**: Automatically detects rational functions of Laplace variable $s$ (or $\omega$) in function definitions ($H(s) = \dots$) or queries.
- **Polynomial Root Finding**:
  - Closed-form algebraic solutions for degrees 1 and 2.
  - Weierstrass (Durand-Kerner) simultaneous root iteration algorithm for degree $\ge 3$ polynomials.
- **Poles & Zeros Extraction**: Yields roots as complex pairs `(re: Double, im: Double)`:
  - Zeros: Roots of numerator polynomial.
  - Poles: Roots of denominator polynomial.
- **BIBO Stability Verdict**:
  - `BIBO Stable`: All poles have $\text{Re}(p_i) < 0$.
  - `Marginally Stable`: Poles on imaginary axis $\text{Re}(p_i) == 0$ with no repeated poles.
  - `Unstable`: Any pole with $\text{Re}(p_i) > 0$.
- **120-Point Logarithmic Frequency Response**:
  - Frequencies $\omega \in [10^{-1}, 10^4]\text{ rad/s}$.
  - Magnitude in decibels: $M_{\text{dB}}(\omega) = 20 \log_{10} |H(j\omega)|$.
  - Phase with continuous phase unwrapping across $\pm 180^\circ$ jumps.
  - Automatic detection of $-3\text{ dB}$ cutoff frequency $\omega_c$.

### 13.2 Bode Plotter & S-Plane Card (`ui/canvas/TransferFunctionAnalyzerCard.kt`)
- **Tab 1: Bode Frequency Response**:
  - Magnitude graph ($M_{\text{dB}}$ vs $\log_{10}\omega$) in Warm Amber (`#FFB300`).
  - Phase graph ($\phi^\circ$ vs $\log_{10}\omega$) in Gold (`#FFD54F`).
  - Decade grid lines at $0.1, 1, 10, 100, 1000, 10000\text{ rad/s}$.
  - Interactive touch cursor readout displaying exact $(\omega, M_{\text{dB}}, \phi^\circ)$.
  - $-3\text{ dB}$ cutoff frequency indicator.
- **Tab 2: Complex S-Plane Map**:
  - $\sigma$ (real) and $j\omega$ (imaginary) axes.
  - Left-Half Plane (LHP) shaded green/charcoal.
  - Crimson Red $\times$ markers for Poles.
  - Amber $\bigcirc$ markers for Zeros.
  - Stability badge (`BIBO Stable` / `Unstable`).

### 13.3 Taylor / Maclaurin Series & Piecewise Functions
- **Taylor Series AST Node**: `MathExpr.TaylorSeries(body, variable, center, order)`
  - Syntax: `\operatorname{taylor}(EXPR, x, a, n)` or `\operatorname{series}(EXPR, x, a, n)`.
  - 1-tap `Series (O^5)` chip in the transformation bar expanding any expression to 5th-order polynomial.
  - Compiled to Symja via `Normal(Series(body, {x, center, order}))`.
- **Piecewise Functions**: `MathExpr.Piecewise(branches, defaultExpr)`
  - Full relational operator parsing: $<, \le, >, \ge, ==, \ne$, and `otherwise`.
  - Disambiguation in `\begin{cases}`: System of equations when rows have `=`, Piecewise when conditions are present.
  - Evaluated in `FastPlotEvaluator` for 60fps plotting and compiled to Symja `Piecewise(...)`.

## 3. Phase 14 — Offline Personal Ink Calibrator & Test Suite

### 14.1 Multi-Stroke DTW / N-Protractor Classifier (`ml/hmer/SymbolPrototypeClassifier.kt`)
- **Resampling & Normalization**:
  - Resamples stroke polylines into $N = 32$ equidistant points along arc length.
  - Normalizes coordinates to unit square $[0, 1]^2$ while preserving stroke aspect ratios.
- **Elastic Dynamic Time Warping (DTW)**:
  - Banded Sakoe-Chiba DTW blended with point-by-point Euclidean metric.
  - Stroke-order and stroke-direction invariance for multi-stroke symbols (`+`, `=`, `x`, `\pi`, etc.).
- **Built-in Prototype Catalog**:
  - Digits: `0–9`
  - Variables: `a, b, c, x, y, z, t, s`
  - Constants: `\pi, e`
  - Operators: `+, -, =, \times, \div, \int, \sqrt`
- **Offline JSON Persistence**:
  - Personal calibrations saved to `context.filesDir/user_symbol_prototypes.json`.
- **HMER Fallback Coordinator**:
  - When `OnnxHmerEngine` is unavailable, automatically falls back to `SymbolPrototypeClassifier` with `RecognitionSource.PROTOTYPE_FALLBACK`.
